THE ALPHA FACTORY

A machine that keeps building trading edges, testing each honestly, sailing the ones that pass, and scrapping the ones that rot. We don't bet on a strategy (they decay) — we bet on the process. Judged on smoothness, not peak return. Places no live orders except the live-paper engines below (Wave Rider + Clockwork), currently in shadow. {% if f.snapshot %}
