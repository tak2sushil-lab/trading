HOW TO READ THIS DASHBOARD

Every term below appears somewhere on the live dashboard. Each row answers three things: what it is (with an analogy — same ones GLOSSARY.md uses in the codebase), whether it can currently decide anything (block a trade, size a trade, close a trade) or only watches and logs, and where on the page you'll see it. Most new instrumentation in this system starts as watch-only for weeks before it's ever allowed to decide anything — that's deliberate, not unfinished work.

THE SIX WORD TYPES

Every term on this page is exactly one of these. Knowing the type tells you what it's allowed to do.

THE EQUITY BOOKS — five books, one real (paper) account

Each book keeps its own table and its own positions, and sells only the shares it bought — two books can hold the same symbol and IBKR shows the blend. The colour bar next to a book's name is the same everywhere on the dashboard.

BookAnalogyMeaningDecides or watches?
Day TraderIn by morning, out by the close The original intraday book (auto_trader.py): catalyst and momentum names graded A+/A, entered after 09:30, flat by 15:45 except a small overnight hold for strong closers. Decides — places real paper orders.
Wave RiderRides a stock already moving Swing book: buys WILD names that popped, holds 3 days, 8% stop. Decides — places real paper orders.
ContrarianBuys what just fell hardest Mean reversion: buys the biggest 3-day fallers, holds 5 days, 15% stop. Long-only, so judge it on alpha vs the tide, not raw P&L. Decides — places real paper orders.
ClockworkBuys the close, sells the open Overnight book: ranks WILD names by how consistently they gapped up over the last 30 nights (ties broken by Night Owl's score since Oct 9 2026, never alphabetically), buys the top 3 (~$3,333 each) at the closing auction, sells at the next opening auction. Its $10k also funds Fear Rebound's day shift. Decides — places real paper orders.
Night OwlA learned model picks who sleeps well Second overnight book (live Oct 5 2026). Same window as Clockwork, a different chooser: a machine-learned model (gradient-boosted trees, ~90 daily inputs, trained on ~11 years) scores every name at ~09:35 for tonight's close→open move; near the close it buys its top 3 WILD names (~$3,333 each, own $10k), skipping names the day trader / Wave Rider / Contrarian hold and anyone reporting earnings, and sells at the next open. Since Oct 6 2026 it may share a name with Clockwork (at most $6,667 a name across both). Re-trained monthly. Out of sample 2018→2026 its top 3 beat the average WILD name by ~18bp a night (positive in 7 of 9 years; 2019 and 2023 slightly negative), with deep multi-month drawdowns along the way. Decides — places real paper orders.
Fear ReboundThe day shift after a fear day Day shift of Clockwork's $10k pool (Oct 9 2026). On a fear day — VIX closed up ≥5% and our universe fell ≥0.5% — the 18:15 Market State Record lists the volatile names that fell hardest; the next morning at ~09:40 it buys the 3 hardest-hit (~$3,333 each, skipping names another book holds) and sells them at 15:30, before Clockwork buys at the close. −10% disaster stop. Idle on every other day (~40 trading days a year). A down day where VIX does not jump (a calm storm, like Oct 7–8 2026) is not traded — those have not rebounded. Decides — places real paper orders.
Basket Tide (Clockwork, Night Owl)The tide gauge for the night shift Bear switch (live Oct 6 2026). If our most volatile liquid names, as one equal-weight basket, closed below their 200-session average last session, the two overnight books take no new entries until it closes back above. Exits are never affected. In replays 2018→2026 it cut the worst drawdown from −83% to −23% for ~5% of the return; it costs money in sharp rebounds (2020) and saves it in long bears (2022). If its data is stale it lets the books trade (fails open). Shown in SYSTEM HEALTH. Decides — blocks overnight entries while OFF.
Ref P&L (Clockwork, Night Owl)Marking to the official scoreboard The same overnight trades re-priced at the session's official close and open from our own bars. The IBKR paper simulator invents auction fills, so ref P&L is the honest read on these two books; P&L is the read on the plumbing. Watches only — written each evening at 22:00 (after the day's bars land), places no orders.

Fish Finder (decommissioned Aug 15 2026)Try every lure Tests every symbol against all 3 regime templates (ADX Trend / Keltner Revert / RSI Revert) every scan on its own signals, instead of one market-wide regime picking a single template for all 241 symbols. SYSTEM HEALTH — AUDITORS (the ones you asked about)
TermAnalogyMeaningDecides or watches?
Trade CopReferee reviewing tape after the game Nightly (`parity_check.py`, 22:45 ET): replays today's trading day through the backtest engine and diffs its trades against what actually happened live. OK = they agree. DIVERGENCE = the backtest and live code have drifted apart — usually means a live code change wasn't mirrored into the sim, or a real production bug fired. Watches only. It alerts (Telegram + this panel) — nothing in the system reads its verdict to change a trade or fix the divergence automatically. A human has to go read logs/parity.log and act.
Mirror BookFlight simulator A shadow strategy that fades (trades the opposite of) the Black Box Recorder decoder's LONG signals. Currently profitable on paper (+pts shown here), but it has placed literally zero real orders since it started — the constitution requires 30+ consecutive green days before "should this go live" is even discussed. Watches only. Not wired to any live capital or decision. Its P&L here is 100% paper.
Crest Watch (futures)Referee watching a winning position On any OPEN, PROFITABLE futures position past a 100pt peak, Claude reads the live 5m chart and logs CONTINUE or REVERSAL_RISK — built directly from a review of manual exit decisions that were right about reversal days but wrong about trend days. Watches only (log mode). Does not touch the trade. Scored weekly against real outcomes before graduation to an actual exit signal is even considered.
Chart Gate / Thesis Check (equity)Referee reviewing the entry / reviewing a loser Chart Gate: on every LONG entry, Claude checks whether the 1h trend supports it. Thesis Check: on any open LOSING position, Claude checks whether the original thesis still looks intact. Both log-only, both scored Fridays. Watches only (log mode). Same graduation bar as Crest Watch.
Field ReportMorning briefing Pre-market mechanical trend/levels (SPY/QQQ/MNQ) + one Claude call synthesizing news into a stance (RISK_ON/NEUTRAL/RISK_OFF), 9:15am ET daily. Watches only. No gate reads it. Scored after ~4 weeks; individual pieces may graduate to an event stand-down or sizing tilt, one at a time, only if they earn it.
