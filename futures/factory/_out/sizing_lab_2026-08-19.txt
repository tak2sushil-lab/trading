  "size down when ATR is high" is nearly the same instruction as "size down in later
  years". If the gain is really a cross-year time bet, it will VANISH within a year.

  Low-ATR days paid more per contract in 4 of 5 years.
  If that is not a clear majority, ATR-normalised sizing is NOT exploiting a within-year
  edge — it is a cross-year leverage schedule, and its backtest gain is a bet that the
  low-ATR years were under-levered. That bet cannot be validated on this sample.
